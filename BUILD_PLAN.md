## Design direction
An architectural editorial aesthetic: warm ivory backgrounds, deep forest green, graphite typography, restrained copper accents, large mixed-case headings and spacious asymmetric layouts. Use technical line drawings, real building imagery as illustrative imagery, fine rules, considered motion and strong typography. Avoid the reference's navy/cyan gradients, all-caps condensed hero, floating glass panels and repeated card treatment. Use a temporary typographic NOBLE wordmark that can be replaced in one component.
